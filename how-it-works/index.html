How Home Renovation Works in Thane, Mumbai & Navi Mumbai

From free site visit to clear quotation and on-time handover — a structured process for false ceilings, modular kitchens, wardrobes, bathroom work, and full flat renovations with Star Art Works' in-house crew.

01

Free Consultation & Measurement

Our senior site engineer visits your flat in Thane, Mumbai or Navi Mumbai. We measure dimensions accurately, inspect plumbing/wiring layout, and discuss your family's lifestyle needs.

02

Clear Quotation & Material Review

We present material samples (marine ply, acrylics, laminates, gypsum) and a transparent line-item quotation with zero hidden charges — approve the fixed quote before work starts.

03

In-House Site Execution

Our specialized team of POP plasterers, carpenters, electricians, and plumbers begin site work — false ceilings, modular kitchens, wardrobes, bathroom tiling, painting — under strict daily supervisor inspection.

04

50-Point Audit & Handover

Before handing over your key, we perform a 50-point quality check (alignment, soft-close hardware, electrical load testing, deep site cleaning).
