Our 4-Step Renovation Journey

We take the anxiety out of home interior renovation with structured milestones, fixed line-item pricing, and on-time site completion.

01

Free Consultation & Measurement

Our senior site engineer visits your flat in Thane, Mumbai or Navi Mumbai. We measure dimensions accurately, inspect plumbing/wiring layout, and discuss your family's lifestyle needs.

02

3D Design & Transparent Quote

We present 3D layout options, material samples (marine ply, acrylics, laminates), and a transparent line-item quotation with zero hidden charges.

03

In-House Site Execution

Our specialized team of POP plasterers, carpenters, electricians, and plumbers begin site work under strict daily supervisor inspection to ensure flawless quality.

04

50-Point Audit & Handover

Before handing over your key, we perform a 50-point quality check (alignment, soft-close hardware, electrical load testing, deep site cleaning) and provide your 10-year warranty certificate.
